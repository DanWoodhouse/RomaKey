ローマ字・かな・辞書キーボード

Both keyboards from the iOS project, now with everything you need to use them all day. Type, and emoji are suggested (sushi → 🍣, ねこ → 🐈). Clear the text and the bar above the keys becomes a toolbar: emoji, 顔文字, GIF and paste. No key ever moves.

ローマ字 · Mode key

Start in

A tap on the mode key steps through:

At least two stay on. Dragging from the key opens the mode wheel, which always has every mode. Left-handed mirrors it: → あ, ← next keyboard. In 辞, the key next to return switches between looking words up and typing English straight in (with the Japanese shown as you type).

ローマ字 · Typing

Hold the space bar and slide to move the cursor (in Both keyboards below).

キー · Key extras

Swipe up on a key for the character in its corner, or hold it. Tap a key here to turn its extra off. ー holds 、。！？〜 (, . ! ? ~ in EN); keys with several choices keep the hold. In EN, holding a letter also offers accents.

候補 · Suggestions

Skin tone

Press delete straight after an autocorrection to undo it. On iPhone, suggestions use Apple's spell checker; this preview uses the 25,000 most common English words.

✨ AI

Answered by

Apple Intelligence (the default) runs on the iPhone, so it only answers there. Claude answers here too, with the same instructions; in the app it uses your own API key and needs Full Access.

かな · Flick

Sensitivity

Both keyboards

Animations: keys press in, the keyboard dips gently when the mode changes, and panels rise into place. Reduce Motion turns them off too. Romaji readings: 猫 ねこ neko. Haptics vibrate where the browser allows it (not Safari); on iPhone they need Full Access.
